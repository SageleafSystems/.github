# Sageleaf Systems

**We build software with people.**

Sageleaf Systems is an independent software development company **helping people turn ideas into working software**.

We work with founders, entrepreneurs, and businesses who have something they want to bring to life. Sometimes it's an entirely new product. Other times, it's a better way to do something they're already doing.

We like getting involved early, understanding the problem, exploring possibilities, and working together to make something useful.

Good software comes from good conversations, thoughtful decisions, and the willingness to build, test, learn, and improve.

## What we do

**From idea to application.** Have something you've been wanting to build? We help you figure out where to start, what matters, and how to get it into the hands of real users.

**Better tools for better work.** When existing systems and processes get in the way, we design and build software that helps people work the way they want to.

**Engineering and collaboration.** We also work alongside existing teams to develop features, improve applications, and help get projects across the finish line.

## What we're building

We believe in making useful software and sharing what we learn along the way. Some of our work is for clients. Some is our own exploration, and some we release as open source.

### [Markdown Leaflet](https://github.com/SageleafSystems/markdown-leaflet)

An efficient, native desktop browser for Markdown files and folders. Built with Rust.

A tool for people who work with Markdown and want a fast, comfortable way to explore their documents.

More projects to come.

## How we work

We like simple tools that do their jobs well. We care about performance, thoughtful interfaces, and software that's enjoyable to use.

We don't believe every problem needs a complicated solution, and we don't believe the first idea is necessarily the best one.

We build, learn from what happens, and keep improving.

We also believe the people using the software should have a voice in how it's made.
